Meet Our Team

The People Who Birth Your Dreams

✦ Founder & MD
Founder & Managing Director

Rachel Elu

"Travel should be stress-free, well-structured, and expertly managed from start to finish."

Rachel Elu is the Founder and Managing Director of Reo Travels and Tours, a results-driven travel company committed to delivering seamless, reliable, and efficient travel experiences across global destinations.

With over seven years of experience in the travel industry, Rachel has built a strong reputation for consistency, attention to detail, and the ability to execute travel plans with precision. Her educational background is equally impressive: she holds a First Degree in Accounting and a Master's degree (MSc) in Hospitality and Tourism Management, equipping her with both financial discipline and in-depth industry expertise.

Having traveled to over 30 countries, Rachel brings firsthand global exposure and cultural insight into the business, enabling her to curate practical, well-informed, and enjoyable travel experiences for clients. Under her leadership, Reo Travels and Tours continues to grow as a trusted brand recognized for dependability, professionalism, and the ability to deliver exactly as promised.
